How a mandate works

Work first. Names after.

You pay for work before any introduction. It keeps both sides serious and lets us put real effort into your market.

  1. Diagnostic

    A short call and a written view of your fit in the market, the realistic buyers and what stands in the way.

  2. Sprint

    Four weeks of mapping, outreach and meetings, with a written report on every account we touch.

  3. Representation

    A monthly retainer and a signed mandate covering territory, protected accounts and commission.

  4. Deal

    Commission on closed business, due on an agreed event such as invoice, bill of lading or payment.

Rules we work by

Out of the broker-chain business.

Physical trade in this region is full of soft offers and intermediaries who own nothing. These rules keep us out of that market.

  • No free introductionsCounterparties are named only after a mandate or engagement is signed.
  • Verified parties onlyWe confirm identity, authority and capacity before we put two companies in contact.
  • No soft offers or broker chainsWe work with principals and their authorised representatives only.
  • Engineers approve substitutionsWe prepare technical comparisons between standards. Your engineer signs off on the application.
  • No side paymentsOur fees are written in the contract and nowhere else.
  • A human decidesOur research runs on AI agents. Every commitment, price and signature is made by a person.
