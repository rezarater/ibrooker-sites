What we do

The partner decides the brand.

In mining, a machine is sold once and serviced for years. The wrong distributor costs you the after-sales business and the reputation with the mine. Most of our work goes into choosing that partner.

Distributor search and vetting

A long list of dealers and service companies already working with mines in your target country, narrowed to a shortlist you meet in person. Every name on the shortlist has been checked against the list below.

For manufacturers without a partner in the country, or replacing one.

Regional representation

We act as your representative for a defined product family and territory: we meet the mines, follow tenders and expansions, and report the pipeline monthly until a distributor takes over.

For manufacturers who want a presence before committing to a distributor.

Market-entry paperwork

We map the conformity route before the first offer: EAC certification under the Eurasian Economic Union regulation on machinery safety (TR CU 010/2011) for Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Armenia, and national certification in Uzbekistan, which is outside the Union. Plus Incoterms, customs classification and documentation.

Our team includes a licensed customs broker and a FIATA-qualified forwarder.

Spare parts for mines

For mines that ask for replacement parts, we compare the offered part against the original specification and drawings. Your engineer, or the mine’s, approves the substitution.

For parts and wear-parts manufacturers serving installed fleets.

What we check in a distributor

Before your name goes on their door.

A company that says it “works with all the mines” is not yet a distributor. These are the checks every shortlisted partner passes before you meet them.

  • Legal entity and ownershipRegistration, owners and the person with signing authority, confirmed from public registers.
  • Brands already carriedWhich manufacturers they represent today, and whether any of them competes with you.
  • Service capabilityWorkshop, field-service engineers and the regions they can reach, seen rather than described.
  • Parts and stockWillingness and the means to hold your fast-moving parts in the country.
  • Mine relationshipsWhich sites they already supply, confirmed with the site where possible.
  • Sanctions and complianceScreening against the major sanctions lists. No dual-use goods. No side payments.

How it runs

Four weeks to a shortlist.

You pay for the work, not for names. Counterparties are disclosed after the engagement is signed.

  1. Diagnostic

    A short call and a written view of your fit: which countries, which mines, which competitors, and what stands in the way.

  2. Sprint

    Four weeks of mapping and vetting. You receive a written file on each candidate and meetings with the shortlist.

  3. Appointment

    We help you agree the terms that matter: territory, exclusivity tied to targets, service and stock obligations.

  4. Follow-through

    Optional representation retainer: account reviews, tender watch and pipeline reporting, with commission on closed business.
