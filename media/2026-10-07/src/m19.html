Letters of credit · UCP 600

The 21-day clock
starts at shipment.

21 calendar days
Day 0Date of shipment
on the original transport document
Day 21Last day to present
and never after the credit expires
On a long multimodal route, check that the original documents can reach the bank in time, or agree a different presentation period in the credit.
Source: UCP 600, sub-article 14(c). General information, not legal advice.iBrooker
